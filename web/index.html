How carsearch works

One search, every source

Dealer inventory, private sellers, enthusiast auctions and salvage in a single query. The same car listed on four sites appears once, badged with all four.

What buyers actually paid

Completed auction results sit beside the listings. Every other aggregator shows only what sellers are asking, which is a hope, not a price.

Deals rated against real sales

A price is graded against completed transactions, not against other sellers' asking prices. Below three comparable sales, no grade is shown at all.

Refine

Price type

Asks, bids and sales are three different numbers. Averaging across them is meaningless, so they stay apart.

Auctions & completed sales

What cars actually sold for. Live auctions show the current bid, which is not a price anyone has paid yet.
