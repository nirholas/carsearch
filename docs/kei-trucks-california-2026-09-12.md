# Japanese kei trucks: California-focused buyer's list

Researched 2026-09-12. Prices and availability change daily; call before driving anywhere.

## Read this first

- **Three kinds of price are mixed in this market.** "On the lot" means the truck is in the US with a title. "Delivered" means the price includes shipping to your door. "Ex-Japan" means the truck is still in Japan and the price excludes ocean freight, US customs, and delivery. Every seller below is labelled.
- **Odometers are usually in kilometres.** Many US listings write "mi" beside a km reading. 38,000 km is about 23,600 miles. Ask which.
- **California road registration is disputed between sources.** Carmanji and several guides say a 2001-or-older import can be titled and plated in California as an imported vehicle. Shogun Imports' listings say kei trucks cannot be registered for unrestricted road use in California. Confirm with the DMV (or the seller's title package) before buying for road use. Off-road, ranch, and farm use is not in question.
- **25-year rule.** A legal import is 25+ years old (model year 2001 or older right now) and comes with customs entry paperwork.
